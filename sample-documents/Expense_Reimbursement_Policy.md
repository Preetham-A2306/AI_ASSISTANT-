# Eligible Expenses
Reasonable business travel, approved client meals, and pre-approved work expenses may be reimbursed when supported by receipts.

# Reimbursement Process
Submit the expense through the company expense portal. Select the appropriate category, attach the receipt, and add a short business purpose.

# Submission Deadline
Expenses should be submitted within 15 calendar days after the expense date.

# Required Receipts
Receipts are required for reimbursable expenses. If a receipt is unavailable, contact Finance before submitting the claim.
