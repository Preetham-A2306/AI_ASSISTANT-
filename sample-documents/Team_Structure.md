# Departments
The company has Engineering, Design, Sales, Finance, People Operations, and Customer Success departments.

# Engineering Team
Engineering owns product development, reliability, internal tooling, and technical quality.

# Engineering Manager
The Engineering Manager coordinates engineering priorities, team rituals, and onboarding support.

# Team Responsibilities
Product teams work cross-functionally with design and sales. Engineers participate in standups, reviews, planning, and incident response as relevant to their team.
