# First Day Arrival
New employees should arrive at the Vijayawada office by 9:30 AM on their first day. Reception will issue a temporary visitor badge.

# WiFi and IT Support
Connect to the company network named Company-Guest on day one. For IT issues, open a ticket through the internal IT Help Desk and include your device name and a short description.

# First-Day Checklist
Complete HR onboarding, activate company email, collect your employee badge, attend the office tour, and meet your manager.

# Office Tour
The office tour covers reception, meeting rooms, engineering workspace, pantry, and the emergency assembly point.
